📖 Operating Procedures

1. Staff Login

  1. Enter your authorized clinic username.
  2. Enter your secure password.
  3. Click LOGIN to authenticate and enter the dashboard.

2. Register New Appointment

  1. Open the New Appointment page from the navigation bar.
  2. The system automatically generates and displays the next sequential Appointment Number (e.g. APT-2026-0001).
  3. Enter patient details: Full Name, Address, and 10-digit Sri Lankan Contact Number.
  4. Select the consulting Dentist from the dropdown list.
  5. Select the required Treatment procedure (estimated cost will be displayed automatically).
  6. Select the Appointment Date (must be today or a future date).
  7. Select an available 30-minute Time slot.
  8. Click Register Appointment. The backend validates all inputs and checks for double-booking conflicts.

3. Search Appointment

  1. Open the Search Appointment page.
  2. Enter the exact appointment number (e.g. APT-2026-0001).
  3. Click SEARCH.
  4. The system displays patient details, assigned dentist, treatment type, schedule, and current status.

4. Generate Bill & Print Receipt

  1. Open Billing from the top navigation, or click Bill next to any appointment in the Appointments table.
  2. Enter or confirm the Appointment Number and click Lookup Bill.
  3. Verify the procedure cost and academic consultation fee breakdown.
  4. Click Confirm & Generate Bill to compute the total and save the bill permanently.
  5. Click PRINT BILL / RECEIPT to open the browser print dialog. Only the formatted receipt will print.

5. Logout

Click the Logout button in the top right corner of the navigation bar at the end of your shift. This invalidates your session on the server and protects patient data.

⚠️ Common Error Messages & Resolutions
Error Message Cause Resolution
Please enter your username / password Empty credentials on login. Provide your registered staff credentials.
Invalid username or password Incorrect password or non-existent username. Verify spelling or register a new staff account via Sign Up.
Username / Staff ID already exists Attempted to register with an existing username or Staff ID. Use a unique username or check with clinic administration for your assigned Staff ID.
Invalid contact number Phone number does not match Sri Lankan telecom formats. Enter a valid 10-digit Sri Lankan phone number (e.g. 0771234567).
Appointment date cannot be in the past Selected date is earlier than today's date. Select today or a valid future calendar date.
Appointment Conflict: Dr. [...] already has an appointment at [...] The chosen dentist is already booked for that exact date and time. Select another available time slot or assign an alternative available dentist.
No appointment found Appointment number does not exist in clinic records. Check the appointment number on the patient's card or search by name on the Appointments page.
ℹ️ Academic Assumptions
  • The system is exclusively operated by authorized clinic staff members; patients do not log in directly.
  • Sample treatment prices and the Rs. 1,500.00 consultation fee are assumed demonstration values for university coursework evaluation.
  • JSON files are used for persistent storage in place of an enterprise database to satisfy the non-framework university software engineering project brief.
  • Data integrity and concurrency are ensured using Java multi-threading synchronizations.
